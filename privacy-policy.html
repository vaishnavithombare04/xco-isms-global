Frequently Asked Questions

Is counselling really free at ISMS Global?

Yes, all study abroad counselling, profile evaluations, and university application assistance services provided by ISMS Global are completely free for students. There are no hidden fees.

What information do I need to provide?

You need to provide your basic academic credentials, passport details (if available), resume, and standard tests reports (like IELTS/TOEFL if taken) to help our counsellors shortlist universities and courses accurately for your profile.

How soon will I receive a response?

We try to respond to all inquiries and messages within 24 hours on working days. If you query during weekends, our senior counsellors will contact you on the next business day.

Can I connect through WhatsApp?

Absolutely! You can message us at +91 95 61 77 66 00 on WhatsApp for quick questions and our support team will guide you in real-time.

Do you help with visa applications?

Yes, we provide end-to-end guidance for visa applications including financial documentation checks, application review, mock visa interview preparation, and pre-departure briefings.
