Frequently Asked Questions

Is counselling really free at ISMS Global?
Yes! All our preliminary counselling, university shortlisting, and application support services are 100% free for students.
What information do I need to provide?
You just need to share your academic marksheets, test scores (IELTS/TOEFL if available), and your preferred study destination and course choices.
How soon will I receive a response?
Our expert counsellors review every enquiry and respond within 24 hours on working days.
Can I connect through WhatsApp?
Absolutely! You can message us on WhatsApp at +91 95 61 77 66 00 for instant queries and guidance.
Do you help with visa applications?
Yes, we provide end-to-end visa application guidance, documentation check, mock interview practice, and post-visa support.
