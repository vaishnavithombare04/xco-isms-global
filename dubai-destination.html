VISA STEP-BY-STEP

Dubai/UAE Student Visa Process

The Dubai student visa process for Indian students usually takes 2 to 4 weeks. It includes two stages: getting the entry permit before arrival and completing the residence visa process after reaching Dubai.

Entry Permit Before Arrival: The university usually applies for the entry permit while the student is in India.
• Standard processing: 10–15 working days
• Express processing: 7–10 working days (if available)

Dubai Student Visa Interview Tips

  • Be clear about your study plans, course, and university details
  • Explain why you chose Dubai/UAE for your education
  • Carry all required documents (passport, admission letter, financial proof, academic records)
  • Answer questions confidently and honestly
  • Be prepared to explain your career goals after completing studies
  • Show proof that you can manage tuition fees and living expenses
  • Avoid giving unclear or inconsistent answers
  • Dress formally and maintain a professional attitude
  • Learn basic details about your university, campus, and course structure
Practice common questions before the interview:

• Why did you choose this university?  |  • Why study in Dubai?  |  • Who is funding your education?  |  • What are your plans after completing your studies?

Validity and Renewal of Dubai Student Visa

• Validity: Normally valid for the entire period of the study program.
• Renewal: Renew through the university before expiration.
• Conditions: A valid passport, evidence of registration at the institution, and a certificate of good health.
If you are a student in Dubai, you might be allowed to work during your studies but you have to follow some rules and regulations of UAE (e.g. part-time work or internships with necessary approvals and permits).

Step-by-Step Dubai Student Visa Application Process

The UAE has a different way of getting student visas than most other countries. Your university pays for your student residence visa. You don't start the process on your own. The university's student services office will start the application process for you as soon as you receive your unconditional offer and pay the first deposit.

  1. Get an unconditional offer letter from a university in the UAE.
  2. Send the university certified copies of your passport, photos, and a medical certificate from an Indian doctor on the UAE-approved panel.
  3. Get an email with an entry permit. It takes 7–14 business days to process.
  4. Obtain entrance visa for UAE.
  5. Undergo full medical examination in UAE that includes Emirates ID bio metrics, blood tests and x-ray of the chest.
  6. Acquire Emirates’ ID and a valid residence visa for the period of study program. The period from acquisition of offer letter to obtaining visa is from four to eight weeks. From accepting an offer to getting a visa, the whole process takes 4 to 8 weeks. Make this part of your plans.
Counselor insight: If you already have a visit visa for the UAE when the entry permit comes, use the entry permit instead of the old visit visa to get in. Getting into the country on a visit visa slows down the process of getting your Emirates ID and causes problems with your student residency record.

Mandatory Medical Tests for Dubai Student Visa

Every international student over the age of 18 must pass a mandatory Medical Fitness Test to stay in the UAE legally. You cannot skip this step, as it is required to issue your residence visa and Emirates ID.

The medical screening takes place at a facility approved by the Ministry of Health and Prevention (MOHAP). The screening consists of two primary parts:

• Blood Test: Done to screen for infectious diseases, specifically HIV.
• Chest X-Ray: Done to check for active Pulmonary Tuberculosis (TB).

If you test positive for active TB or HIV, your residency application will be denied under UAE public health laws. You will be required to return to India. Ensure you are in good health before traveling.

ADMISSION CRITERIA

Eligibility Criteria for Studying in Dubai/UAE

Academic Requirements

• Undergraduate programs: Minimum 50–60% in Class 12 (varies by university and course)
• Postgraduate programs: Minimum 50–60% in bachelor’s degree (varies by university)

English Language Requirements

• IELTS: Usually 5.5–6.5 bands
• PTE: Usually 50–60 score (varies by institution)

Work Experience (if applicable)

• Required mainly for MBA and professional programs
• Usually 2–3 years of work experience may be preferred by some universities

Additional Requirements

• Valid passport
• Academic transcripts and certificates
• Statement of purpose (for some programs)
• Recommendation letters (for postgraduate courses)

What are the Eligibility Criteria to Study in Dubai without IELTS?

  • English-Medium Education: Previous studies completed in English may qualify for IELTS exemption.
  • Academic Performance: Usually requires 50–60% marks in previous education (varies by university).
  • Alternative English Proof: Universities may accept TOEFL, PTE, Duolingo, or an MOI certificate.
  • Valid Documents: Passport, academic certificates, and required visa documents.
  • Interview/Statement: Some universities may require an interview or personal statement.
  • Financial Proof: Evidence of funds to cover tuition and living expenses.
STUDENT LIFE & REALITIES

Life in Dubai/UAE for Indian Students

Accommodation

Students can choose from university hostels, shared apartments, private rooms, or studio apartments. Many students prefer shared accommodation to reduce living costs and stay close to campus. Popular student areas include locations near universities with easy access to public transport.

Part-Time Work Rules

Indian students can take up part-time jobs or internships while studying, subject to UAE work regulations and required approvals. Work opportunities are available in areas such as retail, hospitality, tutoring, and internships related to their field of study. Students must balance work commitments with their academic schedule.

Student Communities

Dubai has a large Indian student community with cultural associations, university clubs, and student networks. Students can participate in events, festivals, career fairs, and social activities to build connections. Indian restaurants, cultural groups, and community events help students feel comfortable while living abroad.

Student Life in UAE: 3 Things That Determine Whether It’s Right for You

First, getting used to people in the UAE is easier than in most places, but there are real rules about Ramadan, how to behave in public, and Dubai's pricey nightlife. Get to know them before you get there.

Second, the UAE does not give students an automatic work visa when they finish school. If you want to live there permanently, you should start by looking at Canada, Australia, or Germany. If you really want to start a career in the Gulf, the UAE is the best place to go.

Third, start the MEA apostille process as soon as you get your conditional offer. This procedure is the main reason Indian students miss their deadline to enroll in the UAE: it takes 3 to 6 weeks.
